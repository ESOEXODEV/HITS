MEET THE CO-FOUNDER

Kevin Prince

Co-founder & Head of Creative

A former Netflix marketing lead, Kevin Prince brings a storyteller’s eye to brand building. At Heads in the Sky, he turns ambitious ideas into experiences that capture attention, spark conversation, and become part of culture.

Kevin co-founded the Los Angeles–based agency with Michelle Ritorto in 2023, after the pair concepted and produced four headline-making drone shows for The GRAMMY Awards. Michelle, a Hollywood production veteran, leads the company’s production.

Together, they’ve built a 360° creative drone show agency and production partner for brand stunts, social campaigns, and tentpole marketing moments—combining creative ambition with the expertise to bring it to life.
